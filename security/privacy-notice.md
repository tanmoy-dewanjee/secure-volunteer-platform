# Privacy notice (draft)


## Who we are

Team C262T-4104, Adelaide University Industry Research Project A01 — Secure Volunteer and Event Coordination Platform. Client: Abhilash Sridhara. The site is a pedagogical prototype.

## What we collect

For the live demo we collect only:

- Account: email, display name, role (Admin or Student)
- Event records an Admin types (title, location, department, times, description)
- Audit metadata when an Admin creates, updates, or deletes an event (who, what, when)

We do **not** collect health information, government identifiers, payment data, or real student records from university systems.

## Why

To demonstrate secure volunteer/event coordination: authentication, RBAC, validation, and audit. Legal basis for a class demo: the records are synthetic or volunteered by testers who signed the UAT consent (`qa/uat-consent.md`).

## Who sees it

- Students see public event fields and their own account.
- Admins see events they manage and audit rows for event mutations.
- The six project members, the client, and the mentor may see the hosted demo during assessment.
- Hosting: Vercel and Supabase (see proposal). They process data under their terms. We use free tiers. We do not sell data.

## How long

Until project handover or 27 November 2026, whichever is first, then we wipe the demo database.

## Your choices

UAT testers can refuse or withdraw (`qa/uat-consent.md`). There is no penalty. To ask for deletion during the project, email the team lead: a3198175@adelaide.edu.au (Cc the team).

## Honesty

The banner says this is a demo. We will not present synthetic volunteers as real people in the report or the talk.
