# AU branding notes (demo)

Tokens live in `design/ui-kit.md`. Palette matched to [adelaide.edu.au](https://www.adelaide.edu.au/).

## Source of truth

Follow the current **Adelaide University** brand (Dark Blue, Bright Blue, North Terrace Purple, Limestone). The 2026 site is not the old University of Adelaide red-and-navy kit.

The geometric “A” in our wireframes is a **placeholder**, not the licensed monogram. Do not export the official logo file into the student repo.

## Do

- Use tokens from `design/wireframes/css/tokens.css`.
- Condensed display headings + serif body (stand-ins: Barlow Condensed, Roboto Serif).
- Bright Blue for primary actions; Dark Blue for chrome.
- Persistent Limestone **Demo Website** bar above the utility row.
- Name campuses as the public site does: Adelaide City, Mawson Lakes, Magill, Waite (not a generic “North Terrace” label).
- WCAG 2.2 AA on text contrast and focus rings for Must screens.

## Do not

- Imply the site is the official volunteer desk or SSO portal.
- Use the coat of arms or the official AU monogram artwork.
- Use old red `#C8102E` as the primary CTA.
- Lock essential actions behind colour alone.
- Ship stack traces in the Student UI.

## Inclusive review

Phone-width explore remains a defect if unusable. Filters stay labelled fields.
