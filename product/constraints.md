# Constraints

## Timeline

Use a real project tool for the Gantt / schedule: Asana, Instagantt, or TeamGantt. Do not use a spreadsheet as the schedule of record.

## Risk

Prefer the host organisation’s risk matrix if they have one. Otherwise pick a published matrix, define likelihood ranges, and use labels such as Likely / Unlikely.

Client questions on risk: see `knowledge/Week 03/week03.md`.

## Budget

The budget in project documents is a realistic estimate, not money the client will pay. Do not ask the client for a budget figure. Infer cost drivers indirectly (see `product/discovery.md`).

Typical lines: salaries at market rates (for example Seek) for each team role over the project duration; procurement; expenses; tax.

## Documents

- Client approval is required before submitting a report. A signature on the report, or a screenshot of an approval email on its own page, is acceptable. That approval page is outside the word count.
- Everything typed in a report counts: tables, figure text (reviewer estimate), appendices, bibliography.
- Submit **one** report document that meets the word limit. Other deliverables (code, designs, extra reports) go up separately.
