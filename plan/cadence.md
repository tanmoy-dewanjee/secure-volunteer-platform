# Cadence

## Weekly load

At least **15 hours** per person per week, including the **2-hour Tuesday** session.

## Tuesday review

Join only the session the team is registered for. Typical shape (durations vary):

- Team meeting
- Meeting with the client (Abhilash)
- Meeting with the mentor (Eagle)

Before Tuesday: finish that week’s progress note and bring it. Work through the knowledge topic for the current dates on `plan/roadmap.md` and apply it to the product.

After Tuesday: schedule a longer team meeting to plan and monitor. Keep evidence of work in a shared space.

## Standing steps

- Team meeting: plan the week; make sure the progress note matches reality; keep minutes.
- Mentor: present the progress note; keep minutes.
- Client: keep minutes.

Progress notes: one file per team per week, named `G4104_Progress_Report_N` (`N` = week number). Each member’s tasks and progress go in that file.

If someone misses the mentor slot, contact Eagle that week and arrange another time.

When meeting the client, minutes go to the mentor before or at the next mentor slot. Bring evidence (repo, designs, literature, board in Jira/Trello/or the client’s tool).
