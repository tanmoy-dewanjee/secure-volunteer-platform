# Weekly contribution

Evaluated throughout the project, mainly by the mentor in the Tuesday slot, with input from the client and the coordinating reviewer. Teammates also review each other. Nothing is uploaded for this; evidence is what you bring to reviews.

Bands below are Excellent / Good / Satisfactory / Average / Poor (20–16.99 / 16.99–14.99 / 14.99–12.99 / 12.99–7.99 / 7.99–0) for each of five equal parts.

## Commitment (20%)

Show up to client, team, and mentor meetings. Excellent: start discussion, share ideas, offer support, raise questions. Poor: miss most meetings and barely speak.

## Contributions (20%)

Evidence of a real contribution to the deliverables, at a quality that matches the band.

## Organisation and planning (20%)

Finish work on or before deadlines; take part in planning and scheduling.

## Initiative, creativity, leadership, adaptability (20%)

Propose solutions, lead when needed, adapt when the situation changes.

## Responsibility, communication, and teamwork (20%)

Own milestones; write and speak at a professional standard; be an inclusive teammate.

Mentors do not mark the report or the demo. Those are marked from the published report and demo criteria by the assigned reviewers.
